HOMELAB - PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 2026-10-30

This policy covers the Homelab app (https://pdenner.github.io/homelab/).

Data accessed   Read-only Google Health data of the signed-in account:
                activity and exercise, weight and body fat, resting
                heart rate, and sleep.
Use             Planning and reviewing the account owner's own training.
Storage         A private database on the owner's own server, reachable
                only over a private network.
Sharing         Readings are sent to third-party AI model APIs only to
                write the owner's coaching messages. Data is never sold,
                used for advertising or credit decisions, or used to
                train AI models.
Retention       Kept until the owner deletes it. Revoke access at any
                time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
Limited Use     The use of information received from Google Health API
                will adhere to the Google Health API Developer and User
                Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
